Usage limits

  • Recommended minimum shutter speeds: 1/50 for static shots, 1/120 for general shooting, and 1/200 for brisk walking or running.
  • Focal length under 150mm recommended.
  • Zooming within a single clip is not supported; RAW video is exempt from this limitation.
